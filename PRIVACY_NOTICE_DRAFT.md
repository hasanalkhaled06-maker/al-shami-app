### Information the apps process
Depending on whether you are a customer, rider or staff member, the apps may process:
- account and contact information such as name, email address, phone number and authentication identifier;
- order details such as items, quantities, price, order type, notes, requested time, loyalty/referral activity and delivery receipt status;
- the delivery address and map coordinates you choose when requesting delivery;
- rider GPS location while the rider is sharing location for an active assigned delivery;
- customer reviews, loyalty activity and push-notification token where those features are used;
- staff or rider profile, role, phone, attendance and operational records; and
- sign-in session information stored by the browser/PWA so the app can keep you signed in.

The restaurant currently accepts cash for app orders; this notice does not describe online card or mobile-money processing as active.

### Why information is used
Information is used to create and secure accounts; receive, prepare and deliver orders; show order progress and delivery location; send requested authentication or order notifications; manage loyalty features and customer support; operate staff and rider workflows; and meet applicable legal, accounting and security requirements.

Precise location is needed to select a delivery address and, for a rider, to share progress during an active assigned delivery. You can turn off browser location or notification permissions, but some related app features may then stop working.

### Who may receive it
Order information needed to complete a delivery may be shown to the staff handling the order and the rider assigned to it. The apps use or are configured to use:
- Supabase for authentication and database hosting (the live project is configured in eu-west-1);
- Arkesel for SMS authentication when that flow is approved and enabled;
- Google Maps Platform for route/ETA functions where enabled;
- OpenStreetMap map tiles and Nominatim for map display and explicit address searches; and
- the browser/device push-notification service when push notifications are enabled.

Providers may process information in countries outside Ghana. Confirm the current provider contracts, exact processing locations and safeguards before publishing this notice.

We do not use personal information for direct marketing without the required prior written consent.

### Retention and security
Information is retained for the time needed to operate the service, resolve support issues, maintain order and accounting records, protect the apps, and meet legal obligations. The business must confirm the actual retention period for each category before publication. Access controls and authentication are used to protect app data; no online service can promise absolute security.

### Your choices and rights
You may ask the controller to explain what information it holds about you, request access or correction, object to or ask it to stop a specified use where the law allows, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to the Ghana Data Protection Commission. Requests can be sent to the proposed privacy contact above after its details are confirmed.

### Updates
The final notice should show its effective date and be updated when the app, its providers, data uses or legal requirements materially change.
